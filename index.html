Questions

Common questions

How much does a cost report cost?

$1,599.99 (low or full utilization) or $500 (no utilization). Flat fee, quoted before we start.

Do I have to sign up for monthly accounting?

No. Flat fee for just the report, or go monthly from $800 with the report included.

When is it due, and what if I am late?

Last day of the 5th month after your fiscal year end. Late reports can suspend Medicare payments. Close to the deadline? Book now.

Can I switch preparers mid-report?

Yes. You sign a permission form, we request your files and plan back from your due date.

What documents do you need?

PS&R, trial balance, payroll, fixed assets, last year's report, MAC letters. The 72 hours start once they are complete.

What if my MAC has questions or my old report had errors?

Amended filings and audit support are quoted on your free call.
MAC questions on a report we filed: no extra charge.

Is the hospice cap part of the cost report?

No, it is a separate filing. We can quote both on one call.
